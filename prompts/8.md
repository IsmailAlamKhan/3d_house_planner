# Prompt 8 — Duplex & Apartment-Shell Features

## Goal
Make the tool understand the specific situation: a duplex built inside an existing apartment space.

## Tasks
1. **Shell setup wizard** (shown for new projects)
   - Step 1: the shell shape. Choose a rectangle or L-shape by entering dimensions, or draw a custom polygon.
   - Step 2: the total available height (slab to slab). The wizard suggests how to split it into two levels and **warns** if the floor-to-ceiling height on either level would fall under 2.4 m, or if the total can't fit two floors plus a slab.
   - Step 3: mark fixed elements — structural columns, beams (stored as an annotation with a drop-down height that is checked against headroom), the entrance door location, plumbing and drain points, and existing windows on the facade (locked openings).
   - Step 4: choose a starting point — an empty shell, a template, or the AI Assistant.
2. **Duplex templates:** at least 4 hand-built templates that adapt to the shell size: compact 2BR, 3BR family, open-plan with double-height living area, and a home office variant.
3. **Constraint checker** (a "Checks" panel that updates live). Each warning links to and highlights the offending element.
   - Headroom over stairs is at least 2.0 m.
   - Stair riser and tread dimensions fall within comfortable ranges.
   - Every room is reachable from the entrance.
   - Bedrooms have a window. Bathrooms are near a plumbing point, with a warning if a drain run is longer than a configurable distance.
   - Nothing obstructs the locked columns or entrance.
   - Rooms meet a minimum area per type (configurable).
   - Door swings don't collide with furniture or other doors.
4. **Level linking**
   - Make the stair a linked object across both levels: moving it on one level moves the void on the other.
   - "Copy walls from level below" lets users start the upper floor from the lower floor's walls.
   - Show the lower level's walls ghosted while editing the upper level (extends prompt 3).
5. **Double-height spaces:** marking a room `doubleHeight` removes the slab above it and adds a railing or glass balustrade along the void edge on the upper level.
6. **Area schedule:** per-level and total area, plus usable area versus wall area, shown in a panel.

## Acceptance criteria
- A new project goes through the wizard and ends with a valid shell, levels, and fixed elements.
- Templates adapt to different shell sizes without overlapping walls.
- The checker flags at least: low stair headroom, an unreachable room, and a windowless bedroom, with click-to-highlight working.
