# Prompt 20 — Real Assets: Free CC0 Models, PBR Textures & HDRI Sky

## Why
About 60 simple procedural shapes can't look like the reference: beds with real bedding, a plush U-sectional, palms, SUVs, a trampoline, gym machines, a jacuzzi. Real 3D assets are needed. They must be **free and legally reusable**.

**Make this prompt idempotent.** Build on the existing catalog and the custom `.glb` / texture feature (`/api/assets`, `custom:<id>`, `tex:<id>`).

## Tasks
1. **Asset sources: CC0 only by default.** For example Poly Haven (models, textures, HDRIs), ambientCG (PBR textures), and Quaternius or Kenney (models).
   - **Verify the license of every single asset** before using it, and record source URL, author, license, and a download date in `assets/LICENSES.md` plus a machine-readable `assets/manifest.json`.
   - Don't use assets with unclear licenses.
2. **Asset pack download:**
   - `assets/manifest.json` lists each asset: id, source URL, pinned version or hash, license, target catalog id or material id, real-world size, and LOD level.
   - An `assets-init` one-shot Docker service (plus `npm run assets:download` for native runs) downloads assets into an `assets` volume, verifies hashes, converts them to optimized `.glb` (gltf-transform: Draco or Meshopt compression, KTX2/WebP textures, about 2K max), and generates thumbnails.
   - Large binaries are **not** committed to git.
3. **Catalog mapping:** every catalog entry can have a `model` (asset id), with the procedural version as fallback (offline, static hosting, or a missing download).
   - Normalize scale, orientation, and pivot (floor-centered) at import. Tests check that bounding boxes match the catalog's `defaultSize` within 5%.
   - Props like bedding color work by **material slot recoloring**: tag recolorable material names in the manifest.
4. **Priority list (everything the reference villa needs):**
   - king bed with bedding and throw (recolorable green);
   - U-shaped sectional, outdoor sofas (straight and curved), sun loungers;
   - dining table for 8 with chairs, outdoor dining set;
   - kitchen cabinets, island, stools, appliances; utility washer;
   - bathroom set (vanity, WC, shower, freestanding tub), jacuzzi;
   - gym (treadmill, rack, bench), sauna interior benches;
   - gaming desk, monitors, gaming chair, LED or neon strips;
   - TV wall unit, rugs, potted plants (several species and sizes), palms, hedges, shrubs, flower beds, small trees;
   - trampoline, wooden play tower with slide;
   - pergola, fire pit, square fountain, waterfall wall feature;
   - two SUVs;
   - garden bollards, uplights, pillar lights, downlights, pendants;
   - sliding vehicle gate, pedestrian gate.
5. **PBR materials:**
   - Replace procedural textures with PBR texture sets (albedo, normal, roughness, and AO where available) for marble or porcelain, light oak, timber decking, stone paving, pool tile, grass, travertine coping, plaster, and carpet.
   - Keep real-world UV scaling, and keep the albedo cap and color pipeline from prompt 17.
   - Pool water: use a proper water shader with animated normals and an underwater light glow.
6. **Environment:** use local CC0 HDRIs (a clear day, a dusk, and a night) as sky and image-based light for exterior scenes and reflections. Interiors still follow the "dark unless lit" rule from prompt 17.
7. **Performance:** use instancing for repeated assets (plants, chairs), LODs for walkthrough, and texture size by quality tier. The reference villa must still run at **45 fps or more at Medium on the RTX 4060 Ti**. Measure it on the GPU PC and record the result.
8. **Upgrade the reference villa** (`reference/villa.spec.json`) to use the real assets everywhere, then rebuild it. Update `FIDELITY.md` with a "visual match" column for each item.

## Acceptance criteria
- A fresh `docker compose up -d` downloads the asset pack automatically, and the reference villa shows real models and PBR materials.
- Turning the asset pack off falls back to procedural items without errors.
- `assets/LICENSES.md` covers 100% of the assets.
- 45 fps or more at Medium is measured on the GPU PC.
