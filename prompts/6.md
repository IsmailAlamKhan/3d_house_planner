# Prompt 6 — Materials, Textures & Lighting

## Goal
Make the model look good and feel real, without heavy assets.

## Tasks
1. **Material library** (`src/materials/`)
   - Use `MeshStandardMaterial` presets with procedurally generated textures (canvas-generated, cached): wood floor (light, dark, herringbone), tiles (square, subway, large-format), marble, concrete, carpet, paint (any color), brick, wallpaper patterns, kitchen laminate, metal, and glass.
   - Each material has an id, a name (i18n key), a category (floor, wall, ceiling, surface), a texture scale in meters, and a color.
   - Apply textures with correct real-world UV scaling, so tiles stay the same size on walls of different sizes. Generate UVs from world dimensions in the generators.
2. **Material picker:** a swatch grid in the Properties panel for walls (inside and outside separately), room floors and ceilings, and item surfaces.
   - "Apply to all walls in this room" and "apply to whole level" shortcuts.
   - An eyedropper tool that copies a material from one surface and paints it onto others.
3. **Lighting**
   - A sun (directional light) with time of day (6:00–20:00) and compass orientation (which way the building faces), with shadows on.
   - Soft sky light with a gradient or drei `Environment` preset (bundled locally, no network fetch).
   - Interior light items from the catalog emit light (point or spot) in "Evening" mode. Limit active shadow-casting lights for performance.
   - Lighting presets: Morning, Noon, Evening, Night.
4. **Quality settings:** Low, Medium, or High, controlling shadows, antialiasing, pixel ratio, and ambient occlusion (drei or postprocessing SSAO on High only). Auto-select based on device.

## Acceptance criteria
- Floor tiles and wood keep a consistent real-world scale across rooms.
- Changing the time of day visibly moves shadows through the windows.
- The Night preset shows interior lights.
- The app stays smooth (at least 45 fps on a mid-range laptop at Medium) for the sample project.
