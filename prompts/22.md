# Prompt 22 — Baked Lighting: Walk Through the Villa Looking Like the Render

## Goal
The owner's wife wants to **walk around in 3D** and have it look like the picture. Real-time lighting alone can't match an offline render. Use the Blender service from prompt 21 to **bake** global illumination into lightmaps, and load them in the walkthrough. This is the standard technique in architectural visualization.

**Make this prompt idempotent.** Requires prompts 20 and 21.

## Tasks
1. **Lightmap UVs:** generate a second UV set (UV2) for static geometry (walls, floors, slabs, ceilings, built-ins, ground, decks, pool basin) with xatlas, either in the app or in Blender. Use consistent texel density by quality: Low 8, Medium 16, High 32 texels per meter.
2. **Bake job** (Blender, through the GPU queue):
   - Bake **diffuse lighting only** (direct + indirect, without albedo) per lighting preset: "Day", "Dusk — Reference look", and "Night". Respect lights on/off and hidden ceilings.
   - Pack into atlases with denoising (OIDN on the bake), store as KTX2 or HDR-capable textures in the `app-data` volume, and key them on **project id + revision + preset**.
3. **Runtime:**
   - In walkthrough and orbit with Realistic lighting, if a bake exists for the current revision and preset, apply lightmaps (`lightMap` on UV2). Turn off the expensive real-time AO and window area lights for baked surfaces, and keep real-time lights for movable furniture plus reflections from the HDRI or reflection probes.
   - Add a **few reflection probes** per level (box-projected cube maps rendered in the app) so floors and glass reflect the room.
   - Movable items (furniture, people, cars) get real-time lighting plus contact shadows, blended to match the baked brightness.
4. **Staleness:** after any edit that changes static geometry or light switches, show the badge "Lighting out of date — Rebake", and use real-time lighting for changed parts until then. Add an option for "Auto-rebake when idle for 2 minutes".
5. **Mobile and low end:** on phones, use the baked lightmaps (cheap to display) with real-time shadows off. The baked look is the main benefit for weak devices.
6. **Verification on the GPU PC:**
   - Bake the reference villa (Dusk) and record the bake time per quality.
   - Walkthrough at **60 fps or more on the RTX 4060 Ti** with bakes on.
   - Capture screenshots from the saved views and compare them side by side with the Studio renders from prompt 21 in a committed `reference/compare-walkthrough.html`.

## Acceptance criteria
- The walkthrough of the reference villa with the Dusk bake visibly matches the Studio render: warm pools of light, soft bounce light, dark corners, glowing garden.
- Editing the design marks the bake stale and the app keeps working.
- The frame rate target is met and recorded.
