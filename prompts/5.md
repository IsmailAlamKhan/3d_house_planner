# Prompt 5 — Drag-and-Drop Component Library

## Goal
A friendly library of doors, windows, furniture, and fixtures that users drag into the 2D or 3D view.

## Tasks
1. **Catalog** (`src/catalog/`): a typed registry where each entry has `catalogId, name (i18n key), category, thumbnail, defaultSize {w,d,h}, placement ('wall'|'floor'|'ceiling'|'wall-mounted'), adjustableProps (schema), build3D(props) => Object3D, draw2D(props) => SVG symbol`.
2. **Procedural models.** Build every entry from primitives in code, with no external model downloads, and make them look tidy and recognizable. Include at least:
   - **Doors:** single swing, double, sliding, pocket, main entrance.
   - **Windows:** casement, sliding, fixed, full-height glazing, bay.
   - **Living:** sofa (2- and 3-seat), L-sofa, armchair, coffee table, TV unit, bookshelf.
   - **Dining:** table (4/6/8 seats) with chairs.
   - **Bedroom:** bed (single, double, queen, king), wardrobe, side table, dresser.
   - **Kitchen:** base cabinet run, wall cabinets, island, fridge, stove/hob, sink, built-in oven.
   - **Bathroom:** WC, basin/vanity, shower enclosure, bathtub, mirror.
   - **Other:** desk, office chair, washing machine, plant, rug, ceiling light, pendant light, floor lamp.
3. **Drag and drop**
   - Library panel: category tabs, search, and thumbnails rendered once from the 3D builders and cached.
   - Dragging onto the **2D canvas** places the item at the drop point. Dragging onto the **3D canvas** raycasts to the floor of the active level.
   - **Wall items** (doors and windows) snap onto the nearest wall, slide along it, and show distance-to-corner dimensions. Refuse invalid drops with a clear message, for example when an opening is too wide or overlaps another.
   - **Floor items** snap their rotation to 15° steps, snap against walls (back-to-wall), and show clearance warnings when overlapping other items.
   - On touch devices, tap an item and then tap the destination as an alternative to dragging.
4. **Manipulation:** gizmos to move and rotate in 2D and 3D (drei `PivotControls` or `TransformControls`, limited to the floor plane). R rotates 90°, Ctrl+D duplicates, arrow keys nudge the selection.
5. **Properties panel:** edit the catalog's adjustable props (size, style variant, color or material, door swing side, window sill height). Changes update the model live.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every listed item can be dragged into both views and edited.
- Doors and windows cut real openings in the 3D walls.
- Invalid placements are prevented with a helpful message.
