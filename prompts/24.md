# Prompt 24 — Layout Engine v2: Good Floor Plans for Any Villa or Duplex

## Why
The current solver tiles rooms with a treemap ("duplex" and "wings" strategies). Results are valid but often awkward: attached bathrooms end up behind bedrooms instead of beside them, rooms come out long and thin, corridors are wasted, and outdoor features such as the pool or garage don't appear (PROGRESS.md, "Verified on Windows"). Every AI feature depends on this engine, so it must produce plans a person would actually choose.

**Make this prompt idempotent.** Keep `DesignIntent`, `applyPlan`, the checks, and the exact builder from prompt 18. Replace or extend `solveLayout` behind the same interface, and keep the old strategies available as a fallback.

## Tasks
1. **Archetype library** (`src/ai/layout/archetypes/`): parametric plan types that adapt to plot and building size, each with zones (public, private, service, outdoor) and circulation rules:
   - **Duplex:** stacked (living below, bedrooms above), split (two halves), and loft with double-height living.
   - **Villa:** central hall with two wings (like the reference villa), courtyard (rooms around an open court), L-shape around a garden, linear with corridor, and compact square.
   - **Apartment shell:** single-loaded and double-loaded layouts that respect fixed columns and plumbing points.
   - Each archetype declares its suitable plot shapes, size ranges, level counts, and where the entrance, parking, and garden go.
   - The reference villa from prompt 18 becomes the reference example of "central hall with wings".
2. **Constraint-based optimizer** (`src/ai/layout/optimize.ts`, pure TypeScript, deterministic with a seed):
   - Start from an archetype's zone skeleton. Place rooms as rectangles (allowing L-shapes for living areas), then improve with simulated annealing or a similar local search: move, swap, resize, rotate, split, or merge.
   - **Hard constraints:** inside the building outline; no overlaps; minimum room width and area per type; every room reachable from the entrance; stairs aligned between levels; fixed columns, plumbing, and the shell respected; setbacks and gates on the plot.
   - **Score terms** (weights in one config file, documented):
     - target area match;
     - aspect ratio (penalize thinner than about 1:1.8);
     - adjacency wishes (kitchen next to dining, ensuite next to its bedroom, sharing a wall with a door);
     - zoning (bedrooms away from the entrance and living area);
     - daylight (habitable rooms on exterior walls, with window length vs. floor area);
     - corridor efficiency (circulation under about 12–15% of floor area);
     - plumbing stacking and grouping of wet rooms;
     - views (living opens to garden or pool);
     - straight, continuous wall lines;
     - optional orientation preferences (for example a sunny garden side, or an optional orientation-rule set such as Vastu, off by default).
   - Run in a **Web Worker**, return the **top N different candidates** (not near-duplicates), and finish within about 3 seconds for a 25-room villa on the owner's PC.
3. **Site planner v2:** place outdoor zones from the brief (pool with deck, gardens, play area, parking with N cars, outdoor dining, garden lounge) using the same optimizer on the plot outside the building. Keep paths between gates, entrance, parking, and garden. Fix the gap where the pool and garage didn't appear.
4. **Furnishing and detailing pass:** after layout, place doors where adjacency says, add windows by daylight rules, run room-type furniture layouts (bed against a solid wall, clear walkways of at least 0.9 m, kitchen work triangle, bathroom fixtures on the plumbing wall), and auto-decorate (prompt 15) with real assets (prompt 20).
5. **Explainable scores:** every candidate has a score breakdown shown in plain language, such as "Kitchen and dining are connected ✓", "Bedroom 3 has no outside wall ✗", "Corridor uses 9% of floor ✓". Users can pick a candidate and say what to improve.
6. **Quality evaluation** (`npm run eval:layout`):
   - Use 30 briefs: duplexes and villas, small to large plots, 1–3 levels, apartment shells with columns.
   - Report hard-constraint pass rate, average score, adjacency satisfied, rooms with daylight, corridor ratio, and run time.
   - Compare against the old treemap solver on the same briefs, and record both in PROGRESS.md. The new engine must win on every metric except run time.
   - Save a contact sheet of plans to `reports/layout-eval.png` for human review.

## Acceptance criteria
- The brief "luxury villa with pool, garden, 2-car garage, gym and sauna" on an 18 × 16 m building in a 34 × 42 m plot gives 3 different candidates. Each has a visible pool, a garage with 2 cars, ensuites beside their bedrooms, and no hard-constraint failures.
- A 3-bedroom duplex brief gives stacked plans with aligned stairs and stacked bathrooms.
- The eval shows the new engine beating the treemap solver.
- All existing AI tests still pass.
