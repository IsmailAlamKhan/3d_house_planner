# Prompt 10 — Export, Sharing & Reports

## Goal
Get the design out of the app in useful forms.

## Tasks
1. **Images:** high-resolution screenshots (1×, 2×, 4×) of the current 3D view, with an optional transparent background and an optional "presentation" style (clean lighting, no grid or helpers).
2. **PDF plan set** (jsPDF plus svg2pdf or an equivalent):
   - A cover page with the project name, date, and 3D hero image.
   - One floor plan per level, to scale (1:50 or 1:100 options), with room names and areas, wall dimensions, door swings, a north arrow, and a scale bar.
   - An area schedule page and a room-by-room materials list.
   - The "Indicative only — consult a licensed architect/engineer" disclaimer in the footer of every page.
3. **3D export:** GLB (via `GLTFExporter`) with materials, and OBJ+MTL as a fallback. Exporting a single level is an option.
4. **Plan export:** 2D plan per level as SVG, plus DXF with walls, openings, and room labels on separate layers, using a small DXF writer.
5. **Bill of materials and cost estimate**
   - Quantities: wall area per material (minus openings), floor area per material, item counts by catalog type, and stair count.
   - An editable unit-cost table (currency selectable) that the user fills in, saved per project. Show totals per category and overall, with CSV export.
6. **Sharing:** "Share read-only snapshot" produces a self-contained `.html` file that bundles a minimal viewer plus the project JSON, so it opens offline in any browser with orbit and walkthrough views. Also offer a plain project `.json` export.

## Acceptance criteria
- The PDF prints at the correct scale (a 5 m wall measures 10 cm at 1:50).
- The GLB opens correctly in a standard viewer, such as an online glTF viewer.
- The snapshot HTML opens offline and shows the model.
- The cost totals update when unit costs change.
